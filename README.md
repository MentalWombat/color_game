# Color Guessing Game

Play at [mentalwombat.github.io/color_game](https://mentalwombat.github.io/color_game/)

## How to play

**OBJECTIVE:**

Guess the mystery color based on its RGB notation, which defines colors by combining the three primary colors: red, green, and blue.

**MODES:**

By default, you play on Normal difficulty mode with 6 circles. You can select a different mode in the upper white panel or play on the default one. Depending on the difficulty mode you choose, you will get 3 - 9 circles to guess from.

**MYSTERY COLOR:**

The second white panel displays the color value in RGB notation. Try to guess the color and click the color circle you think is the right answer.

**START AGAIN:**

If you want a different color, press the 'Guess new color' button. Once you guess the right color, you will see a 'Play again?' option.
